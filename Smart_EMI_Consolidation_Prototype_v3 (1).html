What you're looking at

1
Detect. Module 1 flags rising stress silently — Ravi sees nothing until the notification.
2
Offer. Pre-qualified terms shown before any commitment — before/after framing, not a sales pitch.
3
Review & consent. Explicit consent that funds go to creditors, not to Ravi — the direct-settlement design made visible to the user.
4
Settle. Each creditor paid and confirmed individually — builds trust through visible progress.
5
Live with it. One EMI, credit-score trend, and a milestone-gated path to a better rate and the next product.
Edge cases now covered
6
Verifying. The real decision point after Review — routes to one of three outcomes below.
7
Manual review. Flagged for a human check; existing EMIs continue unaffected while it's pending.
8
Declined. Clear reasons shown, plus an explicit reassurance that nothing about the customer's existing loans changes.
9
Error state. A technical failure (Account Aggregator timeout) with a retry path, not a dead end.
10
Partial settlement. One of four creditors doesn't confirm in time — shown transparently with a clear "what to do meanwhile."
11
Appeal. After a decline, the customer can add documents/context and track the review to a decision.
Use this to pressure-test copy and flow with real customers — the interview guide's Section 4 asks directly whether this notification style feels reassuring or intrusive.
